      How it works

      Six steps. One signature.

      1. 1

        Connect, pick a coin

        Phantom lists every token in your wallet with its USD value. Pick any one with enough value: a memecoin, SOL or USDC.

      2. 2

        Pick your console

        PlayStation (PS5) or Xbox (Series X|S), Standard or Ultimate, and the country of your store account. Standard is $79.99 / £69.99 / €79.99. Ultimate is $99.99 / £89.99 / €99.99.

      3. 3

        Cards that cover it

        We pick the fewest store cards whose face value covers the price, or a cheaper set with more cards. What's over the price stays in your store wallet.

      4. 4

        One signature

        Jupiter swaps just enough of your coin to USDC, straight into the settlement wallet: the cards at Bitrefill's price plus a 1% fee. 0% if you hold 1,000,000 $SHARK.

      5. 5

        Codes on screen

        Bitrefill sells the cards over its x402 API. Your wallet signs one message to reveal the codes here. Add an email and they land there too.

      6. 6

        The fee burns

        Every hour the 1% fee buys $SHARK on Jupiter and burns it. Each burn is a public transaction, listed below.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      Which countries work?

      United States (USD), United Kingdom (GBP) and 20 eurozone countries (EUR): Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia and Spain. Store cards are region-locked, so pick the country your PlayStation or Microsoft account is in.

      What if the price moves during checkout?

      If your quote is old when you press the button, a fresh one is fetched before Phantom opens, so you sign the real amount. If the swap fails on-chain, nothing is paid. If Bitrefill's card price moves before we pay, the full USDC goes back to your wallet and you quote again.

      Is this affiliated with Rockstar Games, Sony or Microsoft?

      No. SHARKCARD is independent. It buys standard PlayStation Store and Xbox gift cards through Bitrefill. Grand Theft Auto, PlayStation and Xbox are trademarks of their owners.

      How do I redeem the codes?

      PlayStation: on PS5 go to PlayStation Store, then the ⋯ menu, then Redeem Code (on the web: your avatar, then Redeem Codes). Xbox: redeem.microsoft.com, or Store, then Redeem on the console. The cards top up your wallet. Then buy Grand Theft Auto VI with the balance. Use an account from the same country as the cards.

      What does it cost?

      The cards at Bitrefill's price plus a 1% fee, paid from the coin you pick, plus the Solana network fee. Hold 1,000,000 $SHARK in the same wallet and the fee is 0%.

      Where do my codes show up?

      On this page, after your wallet signs a message that proves the order is yours, and by email if you add one. Your last orders are saved in this browser, so you can come back and reveal them.

      Which coins can I pay with?

      Any token in your wallet that Jupiter can sell for enough USDC without moving its price more than 15%, plus SOL and USDC. Keep about 0.01 SOL for network fees.
